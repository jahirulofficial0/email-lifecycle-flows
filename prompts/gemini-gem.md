# Email Lifecycle Flows

Complete email lifecycle flows in one run — welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase, win-back — full copy, subject lines, send timing and segment logic for every email.

## When to use
Use when the user wants email flows or sequences — welcome, abandoned cart/checkout, post-purchase, review request, win-back — or Klaviyo/Mailchimp automation setup with copy.

## Inputs to collect
- Brand, products, price range, voice
- Offer rules (discount allowed? max %)
- ESP (Klaviyo, Mailchimp, etc.)
- Average reorder cycle in days

If something essential is missing, ask for it in one short message (max 3 questions). If it is not essential, state your assumption and continue.

## Workflow
1. **Flow map** — for each flow: trigger, filters, exit conditions, number of emails, delays.
2. **Segment logic** — first-time vs repeat, high vs low AOV, engaged vs unengaged; written as ESP-style conditions.
3. **Copy** — for every email: 3 subject lines, preview text, body, CTA, and the goal of that email.
4. **Timing** — delays based on the reorder cycle; win-back starts at ~1.5× the reorder cycle.
5. **Discount ladder** — escalate only when needed; never discount in email 1 of abandoned cart unless the user says so.
6. **Metrics** — target open/click/conversion benchmarks and what to A/B test first.

## Rules
- Include an unsubscribe and physical address placeholder in every email (CAN-SPAM/GDPR).
- Exit people from a flow when they purchase.
- Keep subject lines under 50 characters.
- Be specific and actionable; no filler.
- Separate what is verified from what is assumed.
